VETERINARY CLINICAL RECORDS REDESIGN - RESEARCH NOTES

== SOAP FORMAT (Industry Standard) ==
- Subjective: Owner's complaint, history, symptoms reported
- Objective: Physical exam findings, vitals (weight, temp, heart rate, resp rate), lab results
- Assessment: Diagnosis or differential diagnoses
- Plan: Treatment plan, medications, follow-up, next steps

== PATIENT HEALTH CARD (Digitail Pattern) ==
Top header: Name, Species & Breed, Gender & Age, Weight (with trend arrow)
Tags/Alerts: Color-coded alerts for allergies, conditions
Sections: Preventive, Medications, Diagnostics, Diagnoses
Internal notes: Private notes visible on appointment cards

== KEY FIELDS FOR VET PATIENT PROFILE ==
Required always visible:
- Pet name, species, breed, sex, age/birthdate
- Owner name, phone, email, address
- Photo
- Reproductive status (castrado/entero)
- Last visit date
- Last payment status

== DYNAMIC VISIT FORM DESIGN ==
Always present fields:
- Date (auto-filled to now)
- Time (auto-filled to now)
- Reason/motive (motivo de consulta)
- Free-text clinical notes (SOAP or free format)

Optional fields (added via + button):
- Weight (kg)
- Temperature (°C)
- Heart rate (bpm)
- Respiratory rate (rpm)
- Body condition score (1-9)
- Mucous membranes
- Hydration status
- Medications prescribed
- Diagnosis
- Next steps / follow-up
- Lab results notes

== UX PATTERNS ==
- Dynamic fields: Show a (+) button that opens a picker to add optional fields
- Fields that aren't added don't show as empty — they simply don't appear
- Each added field can be removed with an (x) button
- Auto-fill: When creating a visit, date/time pre-filled to current
- Search: Unified search bar that searches across owner name, pet name, species
- Filter chips: Quick filters for species (Perro, Gato, etc.)
